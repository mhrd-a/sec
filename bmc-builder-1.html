Business Model Questionnaire

Basic Information

Customer Information

Who are the most important customers for your business? Describe their demographics, needs, and behaviors.

How will you establish and maintain relationships with each customer segment?

How will you reach and deliver value to your customers? (e.g., website, app, physical store)

Value Proposition

What value do you deliver to customers? What problems are you solving? What needs are you satisfying?

What makes your offering different from competitors?

Infrastructure

What key assets are required to make your business model work? (e.g., physical, intellectual, human, financial)

What key activities does your value proposition require? (e.g., production, problem-solving, platform)

Who are your key partners and suppliers? What resources are you acquiring from them?

Financial Information

How will your business generate revenue? What are your pricing strategies?

What are the most important costs in your business model? Which key resources/activities are most expensive?
